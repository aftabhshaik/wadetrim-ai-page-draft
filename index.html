ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE

For 100 years, Wade Trim has put new tools to work when they help our clients build stronger communities. Artificial Intelligence (AI) is the latest. We use AI where it adds real value to infrastructure projects: reviewing drawings before they leave our office, making sense of large volumes of engineering data, and helping our teams find answers and expertise faster.

Much of this technology is built in-house by engineers and AI specialists working side by side, so it reflects how our projects are actually designed and delivered. And one thing does not change. Every AI result is reviewed by a Wade Trim professional, and our licensed engineers remain accountable for the work.

Project Benefits

  • More consistent, higher-quality plan sets
  • Issues identified earlier, with less rework
  • Faster insight from large and complex data
  • The right expertise on your project sooner
  • Professional review and accountability on every deliverable

AI-Assisted Drawing Review

Our in-house QC engine reviews complete drawing packages alongside our engineers

Quality control is at the heart of every set of plans we deliver. Wade Trim has developed its own AI drawing-review engine, CivilAI, built around our standards, checklists, and review practices.

Instead of checking one sheet at a time, a coordinated team of AI agents works through the full drawing package. They follow references between sheets, look for inconsistencies, and check the drawings against our standards. A separate, independent AI review then challenges each potential issue against the original drawings before anything reaches an engineer.

CivilAI is being piloted by our engineers on real projects today.

How It Works

1Understand the package
AI agents catalog every sheet and how the sheets relate to one another.
2Investigate
They trace references across sheets and check the drawings against our standards and checklists.
3Challenge
An independent AI review tests each potential issue against the original drawings.
4Engineer decides
Each finding links to its evidence on the drawing. Our engineers confirm, correct, or dismiss it.

Water Resources Modeling

Faster, more transparent hydraulic and hydrologic analysis

Stormwater and wastewater studies depend on large volumes of flow, rainfall, and model data. Our AI-assisted tools take on the repetitive parts of this work, such as reviewing flow-monitoring data and supporting model calibration, so our engineers can spend their time on the decisions that matter. Results are measured against established industry criteria and laid out so an engineer can see, check, and explain them.

AI in Our Design Tools

Bringing AI into the software our engineers use every day

We use the Model Context Protocol (MCP), an open standard that connects AI assistants securely to the design, GIS, and hydraulic modeling software our engineers already use. Engineers can ask a model questions and run checks in plain language, with answers drawn from live project data. Our engineers are piloting these connections today.

Connected Expertise

A century of know-how, easier to find

Our AI assistant helps employees across every office find company knowledge and connect with colleagues who have the right skills and experience. For clients, that means the right Wade Trim expert on your question sooner.

Built With Our Engineers

Practitioners shape every tool

Many of our AI tools begin with a Wade Trim engineer who sees a better way to do a task and builds the first version. Our AI team turns the best of those ideas into supported tools that every office can use. Firm-wide training helps every employee use AI well, and an internal AI Task Force guides where we invest next.

Responsible AI

The tools are new. The accountability is not.

  • Professional judgment comes first. AI supports our engineers. It never replaces them, and final design and quality decisions stay with the responsible licensed professional.
  • Results you can trace. Our tools show the evidence and reasoning behind their results, so they can be checked rather than taken on faith.
  • Your data stays protected. We use approved AI services under Wade Trim's security and responsible-use practices.
  • Proven before relied on. New tools are piloted and tested by our engineers on real work before they become part of how we deliver projects.
